## Dead ends and possible mathematical route
A fixed finite modulus list cannot handle common multiples. Direct FiniteField.Extension over ZMod p had an Algebra instance clash. GaloisField r k works.

Unformalized route. Divide u,v by their gcd d and choose n coprime to d so a projective generator can be raised to the inverse of d modulo n. For the reduced exponents, the generalized Vandermonde det of rows (1,x^u,x^v), (1,y^u,y^v), (1,z^u,z^v) divided by the ordinary Vandermonde gives a curve. Over C, the cover x ↦ x^v+x^u has one u-cycle and v-u transpositions from distinct nonzero critical values. The normal subgroup generated by the transpositions is transitive since the remaining generator has a fixed point on the set of roots. This gives full symmetric monodromy and irreducibility via ordered distinct triples of roots and projectivizing. The off-diagonal curve points with nonzero trinomial coefficient come from these roots by scaling. A finite-field proof would need good reduction and a Weil bound for the twist cycling (x,y,z) under Frobenius.

To sieve for projective generators, try q = r^(3^k) for a good prime r ≡ 2 mod 3. Every prime ℓ dividing n satisfies ℓ ≡ 1 mod 3^(k+1). In torus coordinates X=x/y and Y=y/z, a point whose projective order divides n/ℓ satisfies X^i Y^j=1 for a nonzero pair (i,j) of size O(sqrt(n/ℓ)), by pigeonholing i+q*j modulo n/ℓ. Bézout on the non-binomial curve bounds these points by O(q/sqrt(ℓ)). Distinct ℓ in that progression give sum 1/sqrt(ℓ)=O(1/sqrt(k)). The necessary geometric results were not found in the pinned Mathlib.
